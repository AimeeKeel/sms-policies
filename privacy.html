Privacy Policy

Aimee Keel · Effective [DATE]

This Privacy Policy describes how Aimee Keel ("we," "I") collects, uses and protects information in connection with a personal SMS notification service that sends a daily academic schedule summary to individuals who have expressly consented to receive it.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number — provided by the recipient when they consent to receive messages.
  • Consent records — a record of when and how consent was given, and any later opt-out.
  • Academic schedule information — course deadlines, assignments and exam dates, supplied by the recipient or drawn from sources the recipient has connected on their own behalf, such as a course calendar feed or a course syllabus.
  • Message delivery records — the date, time and delivery status of messages sent, retained for troubleshooting.

We do not collect location data, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To send the daily schedule message the recipient asked for, and to link to the full schedule.
  • To honour opt-out requests and keep a record that consent was withdrawn.
  • To diagnose delivery failures.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who else sees it

Phone numbers and message contents are passed to our telecommunications provider (Twilio) and onward to mobile carriers solely to deliver the messages. They may not use this information for their own marketing. Information is otherwise disclosed only where required by law.

Where it is stored

Schedule data and consent records are stored on private computers controlled by Aimee Keel, not on a public service. Message delivery logs are also held in the Twilio account used to send the messages.

How long we keep it

Phone numbers and consent records are kept for as long as the recipient is subscribed, and the record of an opt-out is kept afterwards so that we can honour it. Schedule data is kept for the academic term it relates to. Delivery logs are kept for a short troubleshooting period.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help.
  • Contact us to request a copy of the information we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.
